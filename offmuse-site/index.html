Private publishing utility

Blogger, without the repetitive steps.

OFFMUSE Blogger Automation is a private publishing workflow that connects Google Blogger to an automated content pipeline. It is designed to help the account owner prepare, create, and publish Blogger posts with fewer manual steps.

1. Connect

The owner authorizes access to Google Blogger using Google OAuth.

2. Prepare

The workflow prepares post content and publishing metadata for a selected Blogger site.

3. Publish

The automation can create Blogger drafts or publish posts through the official Blogger API.

Why Google data is requested

The OAuth application associated with this service may request the Blogger scope so it can identify Blogger accounts and managed blogs, and create or publish posts on blogs that the account owner controls. OFFMUSE does not use Blogger access for advertising, profiling, or selling user data.

Data handling at a glance

  • Google Blogger data is used only to operate the publishing workflow requested by the account owner.
  • The public OFFMUSE website does not receive or store Google OAuth access tokens.
  • Operational metadata such as blog IDs, post IDs, timestamps, status values, and error logs may be stored within the owner's Google Apps Script or Google Drive environment when configured.
  • Google user data is not sold or shared with advertisers.
